## Question 1 - Sorting

### Part 1: How do you sort your data by month?

To sort the data by month in OpenRefine, click the dropdown arrow next to the month column and select Sort. Then choose numbers and sort from smallest to largest. This puts January first and December last, making it easier to compare data collected during different months across all years.

### Part 2: How would you do this differently if you were trying to see all entries in chronological order?

To put all the entries in chronological order, I would sort by year first, then month, and finally day, from smallest to largest. This keeps the dates in the correct order from oldest to newest instead of grouping the same months from different years together.

## Question 2 - Reconciliation

### What is the major benefit of reconciliation? What did reconciliation do?

The main benefit of reconciliation is that it helps match data with information from a trusted source, making the data more accurate and consistent. In this lab, we used the Encyclopedia of Life to find matches for the scientific names of species in our dataset. It helped us identify the correct species names and add unique EOL IDs. This makes the data easier to organize, compare, and use for future analysis.
